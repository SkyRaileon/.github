## We run what we build

- **Built around your workflows.** We map the work first, then build the automations and the AI steps inside them.
- **Connected to your tools.** Email, calendar, CRM and the other software your team uses, on accounts you keep control of.
- **Approval and audit by design.** Anything that reaches a client waits for your approval, and every action is recorded.
- **Fully managed.** Updates, monitoring and daily backups, and changes as your business changes.

## Your data, your systems, your rules

Your data runs separately from every other customer's, encrypted in transit and at rest, with an audit log and daily backups. Neither we nor our AI provider train AI models on your business information, unless you hire us to train your own model. Read more on our [security page](https://raileon.com/security).
